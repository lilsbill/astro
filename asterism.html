Asterism

An Asterism is a pattern of stars in the sky. Can range from just a few stars to a large amount of stars making up a single asterism. It is a much more general term than constellations. A constellation is a specific part of the sky with a fixed boundary. They are assigned by the offical governing organization the International Astronomical Union (IAU). While an asterism is an unoffical star formation in the sky. An asterism can make up part of a constellation. For instance the Big Dipper is an asterism inside the constellation Ursa Major.
