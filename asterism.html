Asterism

Background

An Asterism is a partern of stars in the sky. Can range from just a few stars to a large amount of stars making up a single asterism. It is a much more general term than constellations, it is an unoffical star formation in the sky. Constellations or specific regions of the sky that have been defined. An asterism can make up part of a constellation. For instance the Big Dipper is an asterism inside the constellation Ursa Major.
