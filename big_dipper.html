The Big Dipper

The Big Dipper or The Plough is a asterism inside the constellation Ursa Major. It is 7 stars inside the constellation that together make up a bucket or plough. 3 stars make up the handle of it, the last 4 create the bucket. The Big Dipper is one of the most recongnizable asterisms in the sky due to its extremely simple shape and the fact that all stars can be seen by the naked eye. It also has a counter part the Little Dipper, which is essentially a smaller version of the Big Dipper.
