Alkaid

Background

Also referred to as η (Eta Ursae Major). It is about 5 times the mass of the Sun. Alkaid is roughly 6 million years old and currently lives on the Main Sequence. It is a part of the Big Dipper, making up the "shovel" part of the asterism. The name comes from the Arabic phrase "The leader of the daughters of the bier", with beir translated to rougly corpse or coffin. The daughters of the bier are the three shovel or handle parts of the Big Dipper: Alkaid, Mizar and Alioth.


Luminosity and Apparent Brightness

Luminosity: 576 times brighter than the Sun
Apparent Magnitude: +1.86


Distance from earth

About 104 light years from earth.
