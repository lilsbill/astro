Alkaid


Background

Also referred to as η Eta Ursa Major. It is about 5 times the mass of the Sun. Alkaid is roughly 6 million years old and currently lives on the Main Sequence.


Luminosity and Apparent Brightness

Luminosity: 576 times brighter than the Sun
Apparent Brightness: +1.86


Distance from earth

About 104 light years from earth.
