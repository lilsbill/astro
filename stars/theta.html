Theta

Background

θ or Theta Ursae Majoris is a star that makes up the front legs or Ursa Major. It is a subgiant that is moving away from the Main Sequence.


Luminosity and Apparent Brightness

Luminosity: 7.9 times brighter than the Sun
Apparent Magnitude: 3.17


Distance from Earth

44 light years from Earth
