Alioth

Background

Also known as ε (Epsilon Ursae Majoris). Alioth is the brightest star in the constellation. The name comes from the Arabic word for "the sheeps fat tail". It is a part of the Big Dipper, making up the "shovel" part of the asterism.


Luminosity and Apparent Brightness

Luminosity: 105
Apparent Magnitude: 1.77


Distance from earth

83 light years
