Mu

Background

Mu Ursae Majoris or μ is a star that makes up the legs of the constellation. Sometimes referred to as Tania Australis. Currently in the Red Giant phase of its life and is designated as a M type star.


Luminosity and Apparent Brightness

Luminosity: 1000 times brighter than the Sun
Apparent Magnitude: 3


Distance from earth

230 light years
