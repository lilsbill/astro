Mizar

Background

Also known as ζ (Zeta Ursae Major). The name Mizar comes from the arabic word meaning apron, wrapping or covering. It is a part of the Big Dipper, making up the "shovel" part of the asterism. Mizar is actually double star pair that viewed from the earth without a telescope appears to be one star.


Luminosity and Apparent Brightness

Luminosity: 33 times brighter than the Sun
Apparent Magnitude: 2.04


Distance from earth

83 light years
