Merak

Background

Also known as β (Beta Ursae Majoris) is a star making up the bucket part of the asterism the Big Dipper. One can use Merak and Dubhe to find polaris. By drawing a line between the two stars it points directly to the North Star (polaris). It is a great way to find North.


Luminosity and Apparent Brightness

Luminosity: 63.5 times brighter than the Sun
Apparent Magnitude: 2.37


Distance from Earth

80 light years away
