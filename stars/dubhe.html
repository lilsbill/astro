Dubhe

Background

Also known as α (Alpha Ursae Majoris). Although it is given the designation Alpha, which one would assume means it is the brightest star in the constellation, the truth it is actually 2nd brightest. Logically it would make more sense for it to be named Beta.


Luminosity and Apparent Brightness

Luminosity: 339 times brighter than the Sun
Apparent Magnitude: 1.79


Distance from Earth

123 light years from Earth
