Omicron

Background

Omicron Ursae Majoris or ο makes up the nose of Ursa Major. It formally went by the name Muscida meaning snout or muzzle of the bear. It is a G-type star living as a giant.


Luminosity and Apparent Brightness

Luminosity: 116 times brighter than the Sun
Apparent Magnitude: 3.35


Distance from Earth

116 light years from Earth
