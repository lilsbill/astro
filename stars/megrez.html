Megrez

Background

Also referred to as δ (Delta Ursae Major), it is the dimmest of the 7 stars that make up the asterism the big dipper. Megrez is twice as massive as the Sun but is over 23 times more luminous. Megrez is an A-type star that currently lives on the main sequence.


Luminosity and Apparent Brightness

Luminosity: 23 times brighter than the Sun
Apparent Magnitude: 3.3


Distance from earth

81 light years
