Phecda

Background

Also called γ (Gamma Ursae Majoris), Phecda makes up the upper left part of the "bucket" of the Big Dipper. The star is currently living on the main sequence.


Luminosity and Apparent Brightness

Luminosity: 44 times brighter than the Sun
Apparent Magnitude: 2.4


Distance from Earth

83.2 light years from Earth
